Telecom network design, field surveys and work orders in one place

Network Buddy is a software platform for planning and building telecom networks. Design routes on a map, capture survey data in the field, issue and track work orders, and follow progress on dashboards.

Open the app Contact us

GIS mapping

View and edit network assets, routes and survey points on an interactive map.

CAD drawing tools

Produce and mark up network design drawings alongside the map data.

Field app

Record surveys, photos and job updates on site from a phone or tablet.

Work orders

Create, assign and track work orders from design through to completion.

Dashboards

See project status, survey progress and open work at a glance.

About

Network Buddy is an independent, self-funded product built in Ireland by its founder, Greg Mullen. It is in active development and early testing. The web app is live at network-buddy-gis.vercel.app.
